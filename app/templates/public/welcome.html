Turn long videos into Shorts and Reels, automatically.

{{ APP_NAME }} finds the best moments in a long video, cuts them into vertical clips with captions, writes the title, description and hashtags, and posts them to your own YouTube, Instagram and Facebook accounts on a schedule you choose.

How it works

  1. Paste a link to a video you have the rights to use, or upload the file.
  2. {{ APP_NAME }} writes a transcript and picks the strongest moments.
  3. Each moment becomes a 9:16 clip that follows the speaker, with word-by-word captions.
  4. You review the clips, then they are posted to the accounts you connected.

Your accounts stay yours

You connect YouTube, Instagram or Facebook yourself with the official sign-in of each service. {{ APP_NAME }} only uploads the clips you approve (or set to post automatically) and never posts anything else. You can disconnect at any time.
